Our Story

Leading one-stop solutions

Premier Brands Ink & Co is a leading one-stop printing and branding company in Zimbabwe, providing high-quality products at affordable prices to individuals, businesses, schools, churches, NGOs, and government institutions. We produce high-quality printed materials, branded promotional products, signage, and custom business materials.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers on lead times, printing specifications, and how to place an order.

We strongly recommend high-quality PDF files for text documents, and high-resolution Vector formats (SVG, AI, EPS) or PSD/TIFF files for custom banners and apparel branding. This ensures crisp rendering without pixelation.
Standard photocopying and passport photos are ready in minutes. Document binding, stationery supply deliveries, and standard apparel prints typically take 1 to 3 business days, depending on batch quantities.
